Jai Baba Mulram

Guru Jo Dar

The Abode of Grace, Love and Selfless Service. Dedicated to the eternal teachings of Baba Mulram Saheb.

The Guru Jo Dar Asthan are at Sikarpur, Sindh — Rajkot, Bhopal, Nandurbar, Ahmedabad, Vanthali, Amalner, and Haridwar.

"True devotion lies in serving the poorest, for the Divine dwells within every soul."
"Simplicity and truth are the paths to inner peace and ultimate liberation."
"Always speak kindly and let your actions reflect the love of the Satguru."
