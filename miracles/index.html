Divine Miracles

Witness the stories of spiritual grace, healing, and divine protection manifested by Baba Mulram Saheb.

The Miracle of Endless Sustenance

During a severe famine, thousands arrived at the Darbar seeking refuge. The kitchen had only a single basket of grain remaining. Baba Mulram Saheb sat quietly, covered the vessel with a cloth, and asked the volunteers to serve. Miraculously, the food never ran out, feeding all families until the season turned.

Healing the Incurable

Time and again, those afflicted with terminal illnesses and severe physical pain found comfort at Guru Jo Dar. Baba Mulram Saheb's simple touch, accompanied by holy water and a word of blessing, dissolved the pain and restored broken health, reminding all that faith moves mountains.

A journey of faith, trust, and blessings 🙏✨

Back in 2008, my mother took Guru Naam after an important exam, along with my brother. From that year onwards, our journey of faith truly began. Every year during Rakshabandhan Varsi Saheb, we would visit with devotion and gratitude. Slowly, life started showing us moments that we still can’t forget. In 2009, while sleeping in the hall during a visit, my mother’s silver anklet went missing. In 2010, during a rushed train journey, her gold earring was lost. At that time, it felt like small losses were testing our patience and belief. But in 2011, something changed. Guruji guided us to do langar seva. My mother even offered the money she had kept for travel without hesitation. I still remember that moment deeply—it felt like witnessing something beyond explanation. From that day, we truly learned the meaning of trust. Even in difficult times, my mother always said, “Rakh ta muhnji Guru tae.” And somehow, life kept responding in its own way. Whatever was given with devotion seemed to return in different forms, sometimes unexpectedly—reminding us that blessings don’t always come back the same way. There were many ups and downs in our family after that, but our faith never broke. In 2023, during my internship and job search, I was worried about low salary offers. But I still followed what my Guruji guided—“Join first, everything will grow later.” And it truly unfolded the same way in my career. In January 2024, I completed my internship and started earning. Even today, I often say in my heart, “Rakh ta muhnji Guru tae”—everything is in Guruji’s hands. Whenever I feel uncertain, I pray, “Baba Mulram Saheb, whatever is best for us, please take care of everything.” And somehow, things always fall into place—support arrives, help comes, and life continues. Today, I can say this with gratitude: my journey has taught me that faith is not about perfection, it’s about trust—even in uncertainty. 🙏✨
