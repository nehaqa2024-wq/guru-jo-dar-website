The Divine Legacy

Learn about the spiritual journey, deep wisdom, and lifetime of service of Baba Mulram Saheb.

A Spiritual Awakening from Birth

Pujya Baba Mulram Saheb was born into a holy household in Sindh, showing deep spiritual inclinations from early childhood. He remained detached from worldly luxuries and focused on meditation (Dhyan) and service (Seva).

His life is filled with instances of extreme humility and compassion. Guided by the spiritual force, he travelled widely, spreading the messages of peace, equality, and the importance of remembering God (Naam Simran).

Core Principles of Love and Truth

  • Selfless Service (Seva)

    Serving the poor, the sick, and animals is the ultimate form of worship.

  • Naam Simran

    Chanting the divine name purifies the soul and calms the restless mind.

  • Universal Equality

    No distinction between caste, creed, gender, or status. All are children of the One Divine.

Guru Jo Dar - The Sacred Sanctuary

Guru Jo Dar stands as the physical and spiritual home for followers of Baba Mulram Saheb. It operates as a sanctuary of peace where regular satsangs are conducted, and doors are always open to everyone, regardless of background.

Devotees gather here to sing bhajans, seek spiritual guidance, and participate in community service projects that support surrounding villages.
