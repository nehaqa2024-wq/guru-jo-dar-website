Victory to Baba Mulram Saheb

The history of Shri Masand Guru Darbar dates back many generations. In the city of Shikarpur, in the Sindh province, Shri Guru Arjun Dev Saheb was seated on the 14th Guru Gaddi (spiritual throne) of the Masand Guru Darbar.

With the blessings of Shri Baba Mulram Saheb and his revered ancestors, he assumed the sacred responsibility of the Guru Gaddi and carried forward the spiritual legacy with devotion and humility. Through his service, he brought great honor to the lineage of his forefathers and showered love, compassion, and blessings upon the entire Sangat (congregation).

In later years, Shri Guru Arjun Dev Ji moved to India and established Sai's Darbar at the Bharat Mahal Building in the Marine Drive area of Mumbai. There, while living with his family, he continued his spiritual mission and remained dedicated to serving humanity.

As time passed and his age advanced, Shri Guru Arjun Dev Saheb entrusted the responsibility of the Guru Gaddi to his eldest son, Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji, appointing him as the 15th successor to the Guru Gaddi.

While carrying out his spiritual duties, Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji established a magnificent temple in Rajkot, Gujarat, known as Guru Jo Dar. This decision was made so that devotees and sevadars from all parts of India could gather at one place for worship, service, and spiritual guidance.

Over the years, Guru Jo Dar became a center for numerous charitable, religious, and humanitarian activities. As Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji grew older and his health gradually declined, he instructed his younger brother, Sai Bharatlal Ji, to move from Mumbai to Rajkot and assist in the service of Guru Jo Dar.

Sai Bharatlal Ji never regarded Sai Rameshlal Ji merely as his elder brother. Instead, he honored him as his Guru and devoted himself wholeheartedly to carrying out every instruction with complete faith, humility, and dedication. His sincere devotion and unwavering service deeply pleased Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji.

Recognizing his commitment, Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji publicly announced before the entire Sangat that Sai Shri Bharatlal Ji would become the spiritual head and custodian of the 16th Guru Gaddi. As a symbol of this spiritual succession, he bestowed upon the Sangat the blessing of the 16th Jyoti (Divine Spiritual Light).

Today, with the blessings of Baba Arjun Dev Saheb Ji and Shahenshah Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji, Sai Bharatlal Ji continues to lead Guru Jo Dar, guiding devotees with love, compassion, and spiritual wisdom while preserving the traditions and teachings of the Guru lineage.

Sai Rameshlal Ji had also expressed his wish that eleven Guru Jo Dar temples (spiritual centers) should be established. Among these, Haridwar was his foremost desire.

In accordance with this divine instruction, after Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji attained Brahmaleen (merged with the Divine), Guru Jo Dar, Haridwar, was inaugurated by Sai Bharatlal Saheb Ji on 15 February 2026.

The Haridwar center now features a magnificent Satsang Hall and Dharamshala, which are open to welcome devotees and pilgrims.

  5. Vanthali, Saurashtra
  6. Ahmedabad, Gujarat
  7. Amalner, Maharashtra

With Sai's blessings, more Guru Jo Dar centres are expected to be established in the future.

Sai Rameshlal Saheb Ji also entrusted Sai Bharatlal Ji with several additional responsibilities, all of which he continues to fulfil with complete devotion. Standing beside him in every aspect of this spiritual mission is his wife, Smt. Leenaben Masand, affectionately known by devotees as Guru Maa. Through her unwavering support and dedicated service, she has become a guiding and nurturing presence for the Sangat.

Their son, Prince Sai Prashant Kumar Ji, also plays an active and valuable role in the activities and development of Guru Jo Dar.

Together, they continue to preserve and strengthen the spiritual heritage of the Masand Guru Darbar, carrying forward its traditions of faith, selfless service, compassion, and devotion while sharing the Guru's love and blessings with devotees around the world.

Core Principles of Love and Truth

  • Selfless Service (Seva)

    Serving the poor, the sick, and animals is the ultimate form of worship.

  • Naam Simran

    Chanting the divine name purifies the soul and calms the restless mind.

  • Universal Equality

    No distinction between caste, creed, gender, or status. All are children of the One Divine.

Guru Jo Dar - The Sacred Sanctuary

Guru Jo Dar stands as the physical and spiritual home for followers of Baba Mulram Saheb. It operates as a sanctuary of peace where regular satsangs are conducted, and doors are always open to everyone, regardless of background.

Devotees gather here to sing bhajans, seek spiritual guidance, and participate in community service projects that support surrounding villages.
